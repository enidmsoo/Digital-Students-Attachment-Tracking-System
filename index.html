Digital Student Attachment Tracking System

A web-based system designed to simplify the management, monitoring and tracking of student attachment activities. It connects students and university supervisors through a centralised digital platform.

Explore the System

About the Project

The Digital Student Attachment Tracking System provides an organised way of managing student attachment records, monitoring progress, maintaining logbooks and coordinating communication between students and university supervisors.

System Features

Student Portal

Allows students to access their accounts and manage attachment activities.

Supervisor Portal

Enables supervisors to monitor students and review attachment progress.

Digital Logbook

Allows students to record their daily attachment activities.

Progress Tracking

Helps monitor student attachment progress and completion.

Appointment Management

Supports coordination between students and supervisors.

Reports

Provides organised attachment information and progress reports.

Technologies Used

HTML | CSS | JavaScript | PHP | MySQL | XAMPP
